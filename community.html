EVERYONE'S RESPECT.

DIGO is an early-stage social chat and mini-games website. The community is still small and the features are being tested. Read this before chatting with other people.

✳ Be a good human

  • Respect others. No harassment, hate, threats, stalking or bullying.
  • No sexual exploitation, explicit content involving minors, or requests for intimate photos.
  • No impersonation, scams, spam, malicious links or attempts to steal accounts.
  • No sharing another person's address, phone number or personal information without permission.
  • Don't pressure people for money or to meet offline. Block and report suspicious behaviour.

🛡 Your safety controls

  • Tap Report on a public-room message to flag abuse for review.
  • Tap Block to hide another person and restrict interaction.
  • Only approved mutual friends can exchange direct messages.
  • Moderators can review reports, remove reported content and suspend accounts.
  • Moderation is currently handled by a small beta team; reports are not monitored around the clock.

🔎 Privacy basics

You join with a guest nickname and color ID. DIGO creates an anonymous Supabase account ID and stores its login session in your browser. Clearing browser data or signing out may permanently lose that guest identity.

Public rooms are not private. Other room participants can read your messages and usernames. Friend-only DMs are not end-to-end encrypted. Do not post passwords, contact details or sensitive information.

Chat messages, reactions, friendships and reports are stored through Supabase. Cloudflare Turnstile checks new guest logins. GitHub Pages hosts the website. We do not ask for a phone number or email for guest signup.

Deleted chat content may be replaced by a marker, while the original text of a reported message can be retained privately as moderation evidence. A local Clear for me action does not erase server history.

🎮 Beta expectations

  • DIGO is free during the beta and may change or experience interruptions.
  • Report bugs to the project owner through the DIGO GitHub project; don't include private data in public issues.
  • Do not treat other members as verified or assume a nickname matches a real-world identity.
  • There is currently no self-service account export or deletion screen; these remain launch-readiness work.
  • Additional child-safety, privacy and account recovery controls are needed before large-scale or youth-focused promotion.
Need help? Use a message's Report option for inappropriate content. For immediate danger, contact appropriate local emergency services rather than relying on DIGO moderation. For product questions and non-sensitive feedback, visit the DIGO GitHub project.
